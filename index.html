Volume scans from the public NOAA NEXRAD Level II archive (unidata-nexrad-level2). Each gate is drawn at its beam height; tilt the map (right-drag) to see the storm structure. Hover a point for its value. Add stations to show several radars at once; with the lock on, stepping one snaps the others to their nearest scan. Birds mode keeps reflectivity where ρHV < 0.95 (not rain), as birds/km³ (11 cm² per bird), out to the chosen range, with a per-height profile and VAD ground speed and heading from gates 5–40 km out. Far gates are drawn but undercount: the beam overshoots and outgrows the migration layer. With forcings loaded, the profile shows the rain and 10 m wind at the radar. Use night scans, 2–4 h after sunset; without winds aloft, insects can't be told apart.
